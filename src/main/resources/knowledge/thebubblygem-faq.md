## General Information

### What is TheBubblyGem?

TheBubblyGem is a clothing brand offering stylish fashion pieces
designed for elegant everyday wear.

### Where is TheBubblyGem located?

TheBubblyGem Pte Ltd is located at TBG @ T-Space, 1 Tampines North Drive
1, T-Space, #02-23, Singapore 528559.

### When is self-collection available?

Self-collection is available Friday to Sunday, from 12 PM to 6 PM.
Customers should collect only after receiving an email confirming that
the parcel is ready.

## Shipping and Delivery

### How long does local delivery take?

Local delivery generally takes 1 to 3 working days.

### How does local delivery pickup work?

Local delivery pickups take place daily. The cutoff time is 3 PM; orders
after 3 PM are picked up the next day.

### How much does local shipping cost?

The FAQ lists a flat local shipping rate of SGD 6.

### Is free local delivery available?

The website currently advertises free delivery for a single order above
SGD 150.

### How much is shipping to Malaysia?

The FAQ lists 46 RM for 1 to 2 pieces and 56 RM for 2 to 3 pieces. A
separate shipping page states that Malaysia delivery may take 7 to 14
working days because of customs checks.

### How much is shipping to Brunei?

The FAQ lists 30 BND for 1 to 2 pieces and 34 BND for 2 to 3 pieces.

### How much is shipping to Australia?

The FAQ lists 42 AUD for 1 to 2 pieces and 50 AUD for 2 to 3 pieces.

### Does TheBubblyGem ship internationally?

Yes. International shipping is available to selected countries. Delivery
times vary, and customers may be responsible for applicable customs
duties, taxes, or fees.

### How can I track my order?

After an order is shipped, the customer receives an email containing a
tracking number and tracking link.

### Can I change an order after placing it?

Customers should contact TheBubblyGem as soon as possible. Changes may
no longer be possible after the order has been processed. Instagram
direct message is also suggested for a faster response.

the cart, and proceed through checkout.

TheBubblyGem accepts options including credit or debit cards, online

### Does TheBubblyGem sell gift cards?

## Product Information and Sizing

### What sizes does TheBubblyGem carry?

The FAQ lists S (PTP 45), M (PTP 50), L (PTP 55), and XL (PTP 60).
Customers should also check the measurements in each product description
because product sizing may vary.

### Where can I find product measurements?

Measurements and size information are provided in the relevant product
description or size chart.

### Are TheBubblyGem dresses lined?

The FAQ says dresses have lining, but customers should check the
individual product description for product-specific details.

### Where can I find detailed product information?

Detailed descriptions and images are available on individual product
pages. Customers can contact TheBubblyGem if they need clarification
about a particular product.

## Returns, Exchanges and Refunds

### What is the return or exchange policy?

The published policy states that an item may be exchanged within 7 days
from purchase when a manufacturing defect is found. Exchanges take place
during operational hours and depend on item availability.

### Can I exchange an item because I bought the wrong size?

No. The FAQ says exchanges are not available simply because the wrong
size was purchased. Customers are encouraged to review product
measurements before ordering or visit the studio to try items where
possible.

### Can sale items be returned, exchanged, or refunded?

No. Sale items are final. The published policy states that sale items
are not eligible for exchanges, returns, or refunds for size or design
reasons.

### What condition must an item be in for an eligible exchange or refund?

Eligible items must retain their original packaging, tags, and labels.
Proof of purchase is required.

### How long can a refund take to process?

The published policy says to allow up to 7 working days for refund
processing.

### What should I do when collecting an item myself?

Customers should inspect self-pickup items while at the studio. The
published policy says TheBubblyGem will not be liable for defects
reported only after the customer has left the studio.

### Can TheBubblyGem reject a return or exchange?

Yes. The published terms state that TheBubblyGem may reject requests
that do not meet its stated requirements and may amend its terms and
conditions.

## Damaged Items

### What should I do if an item arrives damaged?

Contact TheBubblyGem within 48 hours of receiving the item and provide
clear photographs of the damage. Depending on the circumstances, a
replacement or refund may be arranged.

## Loyalty and Gifts

### Does TheBubblyGem have a loyalty points program?

Yes. Customers can earn points from purchases and redeem them for
discounts on future orders. The website should be checked for current
earning and redemption details.

### Is gift packaging available?

Yes. Customers who want gift wrapping should indicate this in the order
remarks during checkout.
